Privacy Policy

Ribbon · Effective date: September 30, 2026

In short: Ribbon is a desktop app that opens the PDF books in your Google Drive and remembers which page you reached. It talks directly to Google from your computer. The developer has no server, does not receive your data, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your information.

1. Who we are

Ribbon (“the app”) is developed by RAJEEVAN. You can contact us at naveejr@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Ribbon desktop application and this website, [YOUR DOMAIN].

2. What Ribbon does

After you sign in with Google, Ribbon lets you search the PDF files in your Google Drive, add books to a reading list, open them, and save your reading position so you can continue on another computer.

3. Information Ribbon accesses

InformationWhy Ribbon needs it
Your Google account email addressTo show which account is signed in.
Names and details (size, modified time, checksum) of PDF files in your DriveTo search for books by name and to detect when a downloaded copy is out of date.
The contents of PDF files you choose to openTo display the book on your screen.
Your reading progress: book identifier and name, current page, total pages, last update timeTo resume where you stopped, on this or another computer.

Ribbon does not read, list, or change any other type of file in your Drive, and it never modifies, moves, or deletes your PDFs.

4. Google permissions (scopes) and why

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly – read-only access so Ribbon can search for and download the PDF books you choose. Ribbon cannot change your files.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata – access to a hidden folder in your Drive that only Ribbon can see, used to store one small file (progress.json) holding your reading progress.
  • openid and email – to identify the signed-in account.

5. Where your data is stored

  • In your Google Drive: the progress file in Ribbon’s hidden app folder.
  • On your computer: your sign-in token (encrypted using your operating system’s secure storage where available), a local copy of your reading list and progress, and cached copies of PDFs you have opened so they load faster and work offline.
  • Not on our servers: Ribbon has no backend operated by the developer. Your Google data goes only between your computer and Google.

6. Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not collect your Google user data, and therefore we do not share it, sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence models. Ribbon contains no analytics or advertising components. No human reads your data.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Ribbon’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Sign out in the app: the app revokes its Google access and deletes the stored sign-in token from your computer.
  • Remove a book from your reading list: it disappears from the list and its progress is marked removed. Your PDF in Drive is never touched.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app and delete its data folder (Linux: ~/.config/Ribbon; Windows: %APPDATA%\Ribbon).
  • Delete data in Drive: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find Ribbon, and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove Ribbon’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot retrieve or delete it on your behalf; the steps above are the way to remove it.

9. Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. Sign-in happens in your normal web browser using Google’s OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and Ribbon never sees your Google password. Your sign-in token is stored encrypted using your operating system’s secure storage where the system provides it. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please also protect your computer with a screen lock and account password.

10. Children

Ribbon is a general-purpose reader and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Ribbon handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how Google user data is used, we will ask for your consent again where required.
